Who we are

A home where children are seen, safe and loved.

Amitabha Foundation Children Home is part of the Amitabha Foundation family of care. We welcome children who cannot safely live at home, whether through poverty, loss or difficult family circumstances, and walk with them until they are ready to stand on their own.

Safety first, always

Clear child-protection practices, trained caregivers and a calm, secure home.

Family, not an institution

Shared meals, birthdays and festivals, celebrated together like any family.

Transparent giving

Every donation is tax-deductible and traceable to the care it funds.
